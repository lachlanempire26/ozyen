Best way to get yen in Australia

Last reviewed 5 November 2026

There is no single best way. What matters is how many yen you end up with for your Australian dollars, after the exchange rate margin and every fee.

Start with the mid-market rate

The mid-market rate is the midpoint between buy and sell prices on global markets. Providers add a margin to it, and sometimes a fee on top. The gap between the mid-market rate and what you receive is the real cost. OzYen shows the mid-market rate as the benchmark, from Frankfurter daily reference rates.

The main options

  • Card or travel-card app. You spend yen directly. Check the foreign transaction fee and the rate margin.
  • Cash from ATMs in Japan. You pay the Japanese ATM operator's fee and possibly your own bank's fee. See our Japan ATM guide.
  • Cash exchanged before you leave. Usually convenient, often the widest margin, especially at airport kiosks.
  • Bank or money transfer. Best for larger amounts or paying someone in Japan. Fixed fees weigh more on small amounts and less on big ones.

Two habits that save money

  • Compare what the recipient gets, not the headline rate.
  • When a card machine or ATM in Japan offers to charge you in Australian dollars, choose yen. The machine's own conversion is usually worse than your card's.
