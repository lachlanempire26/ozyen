How much cash to take to Japan

Last reviewed 5 November 2026

Japan is far more card-friendly than it used to be, but it is not fully cashless. Japan's cashless payment ratio reached 58% in 2025, according to figures from the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry.

A sensible plan

  • Carry enough cash for a day or two of ordinary spending, then top up from ATMs as you go.
  • Cards usually work in cities: hotels, department stores, chain restaurants and convenience stores.
  • Bring a mix of payment methods: a card, some cash and a transit IC card (see our Suica and Pasmo guide).

Where cash still matters

Temples and shrines, small restaurants, some ticket machines, some taxis, and rural areas. The further you go from big cities, the more cash you should carry.

Carrying large amounts

  • Japan requires you to declare cash and similar items over ¥1,000,000 when you arrive.
  • Australia has its own reporting rule for large amounts of physical currency. Check the current threshold with AUSTRAC before you travel.
