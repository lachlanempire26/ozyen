Japan ATM fees for Australians

Last reviewed 5 November 2026

Cash is still useful in Japan, and convenience store and post office ATMs take foreign cards. Limits and fees change, so treat the numbers below as a guide and read the ATM screen before you confirm.

Limits per withdrawal

  • Seven Bank (7-Eleven): Seven Bank set the limit for overseas-issued chip cards at ¥100,000 per withdrawal, and ¥30,000 for magnetic-stripe cards.
  • Japan Post Bank: commonly reported as ¥50,000 per withdrawal.
  • Your own bank sets a daily limit too. It can be lower than the ATM's.

Fees

Two fees can apply: the ATM operator's fee in Japan, and your own bank's overseas fee. Reported operator fees at bank ATMs range from about ¥110 up to ¥660 per withdrawal, with some ATMs free depending on your card and the time of day. The fee is per transaction, so fewer, larger withdrawals usually cost less.

Tips

  • Test a withdrawal early in your trip, while you still have other ways to pay.
  • Choose "yen" if the ATM asks which currency to charge.
  • Do not retry a declined withdrawal several times. Check your card's overseas and cash limits first.
