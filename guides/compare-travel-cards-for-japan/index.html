How to compare travel cards for Japan (Wise, Revolut and others)

Last reviewed 5 November 2026

Travel cards and money apps (Wise, Revolut and others) can be cheaper than a bank card in Japan, but fees, limits and rates differ and change. This page shows how to compare them. We do not state a provider's fees here until we have verified them on the date shown on our comparison.

Checklist

  • Exchange rate margin. How far is the rate from the mid-market rate?
  • Card spending fee. Is there a foreign transaction fee on purchases in yen?
  • ATM fees and limits. How much can you withdraw free each month, and what happens after? Japan's ATM operators may charge their own fee too.
  • Weekend rates. Some providers widen the margin when markets are closed.
  • Top-up cost. What does it cost to load the card from your Australian account?
  • Acceptance. Visa and Mastercard are the safest networks to rely on in Japan.
  • Setup time. Identity checks can take days. Do it before you fly.

Do the sum

Work out what you will really spend: card payments, cash withdrawals and top-ups. Then compare the total cost, not the headline rate. Our comparison does this for transfer amounts.
