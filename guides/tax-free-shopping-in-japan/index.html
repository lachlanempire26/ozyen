Tax-free shopping in Japan (new rules from 1 November 2026)

Last reviewed 5 November 2026

From 1 November 2026, Japan's tax-free system for visitors switched to a refund method. You now pay the full price including the 10% consumption tax at the till, then claim the tax back when you leave Japan.

What changed

  • Before: eligible visitors bought goods without the consumption tax at the till.
  • Now: you pay the tax-inclusive price, then apply for the refund at customs on departure, before you check in for your flight.
  • Purchases made on or before 31 October 2026 follow the old rules, even if you fly home in November.

What stayed the same

  • Purchases must total at least ¥5,000 before tax at the same store on the same day.
  • Visitors are still advised to get a passport stamp on arrival (ask for one straight after the automated gates).
  • There is still a departure procedure and possible inspection.

Practical tips

  • Keep your passport handy when you shop.
  • Keep receipts and goods organised by purchase.
  • Do not use or consume tax-free consumables in Japan if you want the refund.
  • Expect queues while the new system settles in.

Details such as how the refund is paid can change. Check the official Japan National Tourism Organization pages before you shop.
