Suica and Pasmo top-ups

Last reviewed 5 November 2026

Suica and Pasmo are prepaid IC cards for trains, buses, vending machines and many shops. Since 2007 they have been interoperable, so for visitors the real choice is how you get one, not which brand.

Your options

  • Welcome Suica (physical card). No deposit. It expires 28 days after first use, and any balance left on it is lost, so top up only what you will spend.
  • Suica in Apple Wallet or the Welcome Suica Mobile app (iPhone). JR East launched the app in March 2025. You top up with a card through Apple Pay.
  • Android. Most Android phones sold outside Japan lack the FeliCa chip mobile Suica needs, so a physical card is the safer plan. JR East has announced Samsung Galaxy support for overseas visitors, planned for the first half of 2027.

Topping up

  • Physical cards: top up with cash at station machines or convenience store registers, in ¥1,000 steps.
  • Phone cards: top up in the wallet or app with a credit card.

Rules and apps change often. Check JR East's official pages before you travel.
