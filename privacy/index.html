Privacy

Last updated 5 November 2026

OzYen is operated by a sole trader in Australia.

What we collect

OzYen does not ask you to create an account. The amounts you type into the calculator stay in your browser and are not sent to us.

Analytics

We do not currently run analytics.

Third parties

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it serves them. The live exchange rate is requested from Frankfurter. When you click a provider link, you leave OzYen and that provider's own privacy policy applies. Some links are affiliate links, so the provider may tell us that you came from OzYen.

Language preference

OzYen may store your language choice in your browser (local storage). It is not sent to us.

Your rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. We do not sell personal information.

General information only, not financial advice. OzYen does not consider your objectives or circumstances. Rates are indicative.

Some links are affiliate links (marked Sponsored). We may earn a commission. Ranking is by amount received, never by commission.

OzYen is a comparison site. It does not hold, move or exchange money.
